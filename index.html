Client Discretion & Policies

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At The Fein Heit Boutique, we hold client privacy with the utmost reverence. We never sell, lease, or distribute your personal details, skin consultation histories, or purchasing records to any third party.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only essential details necessary to curate your bespoke luxury experience:

  • Contact Details: Name, email address, and phone number for digital booking receipts and live shipment tracking notifications.
  • Delivery Addresses: Street address and city for white-glove doorstep delivery.
  • Skin Profile & Preferences: Consultation notes, allergies, or fragrance preferences voluntarily shared to personalize treatments.

3. 256-Bit SSL Security & Payment Safety

All online payment transactions and tracking inquiries are encrypted through bank-grade 256-bit SSL protocols. Payment credentials are processed directly through certified PCI-compliant gateways.
